AabRahat Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

AabRahat ("we", "our", "the app") provides on-demand water tanker delivery, IoT tank monitoring, and related logistics services through our mobile apps and admin portal. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

Account information: name, email address, phone number, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text).

Customer profile: full name, house/unit number, property portion, delivery address, and GPS coordinates for that address.

Driver profile (drivers only): CNIC/national ID number, driving license number, date of birth, emergency contact, and vehicle information, collected during onboarding by an administrator.

Location: your device's location is used to set an accurate delivery address, and (for drivers) to share live location with dispatch and the customer only while a delivery is actively in progress. We do not track location in the background or outside the app.

Camera: used only to scan QR invite codes for sharing device access with another person (e.g. a tenant). Camera images are processed on-device to read the code and are not stored or uploaded.

Order & device data: order history, delivery status, ratings, IoT water-tank sensor readings, and consumption history for properties you own or have been granted access to.

Payment information: card and payment details are collected and processed directly by our payment processor, Stripe; we do not store full card numbers on our own servers.

Support interactions: messages you send through in-app support/complaints, which may be assisted by an AI support tool used by our call-center staff.

How we use this information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you
  • To process and deliver your water orders, and dispatch the nearest available driver
  • To monitor your IoT tank device and send low-water or predictive reorder notifications
  • To process payments and maintain transaction records
  • To send order, delivery, and account notifications via push and, for time-sensitive events, SMS
  • To respond to support requests and complaints
  • To maintain security and audit logs of account and administrative actions

Who we share information with

We share information only as needed to operate the service:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Twilio — SMS delivery for critical, time-sensitive notifications
  • Expo — push notification delivery
  • Anthropic — powers an AI assistant used by our call-center staff for support troubleshooting
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (AWS, MongoDB Atlas) — hosting and data storage

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data retention

We retain account and order data for as long as your account is active. Order, payment, and audit-log records are kept after account deletion as required to comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations. See our Delete Account page for the full breakdown of what's deleted immediately versus retained, and how to request deletion.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile information at any time in the app.
  • You can control push and SMS notification preferences in Settings.
  • You can delete your account, or request deletion of a driver/staff account, via our Delete Account page.
